Reading List

Foundational textbooks for machine learning, optimization, physics-based deep learning and computer graphics. Hover over a book to see what it covers.

  • ML Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning Christopher M. Bishop Machine learning, the Bayesian way: graphical models, kernels and mixture models.microsoft.com↗
  • Data Principles of Data Mining David Hand, Heikki Mannila and Padhraic Smyth Models, patterns and the algorithms that find them, from statistics and computing.box.cs.istu.ru↗
  • Algorithms Randomized Algorithms Rajeev Motwani and Prabhakar Raghavan Randomness as a tool: Las Vegas and Monte Carlo algorithms, tail bounds, random walks.cambridge.org↗
  • Optimization Convex Optimization Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe Recognising and solving convex problems: duality and interior-point methods.web.stanford.edu↗
  • Physics Physics-based Deep Learning N. Thuerey, P. Holl, M. Mueller, P. Schnell, F. Trost, K. Um Deep learning meets simulation: physical losses and differentiable solvers.physicsbaseddeeplearning.org↗
  • ML Hands‑On Machine Learningwith Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow Aurélien Géron Practical ML in Python, from scikit-learn to deep networks in Keras and TensorFlow.oreilly.com↗
  • AI Artificial IntelligenceA Modern Approach Stuart J. Russell and Peter Norvig The standard AI text: search, logic, planning, uncertainty and learning.aima.cs.berkeley.edu↗
  • Vision Digital Image Processing Rafael C. Gonzalez and Richard E. Woods Enhancement, filtering, the frequency domain, restoration and segmentation.pearson.com↗
  • RL Deep Reinforcement Learning Hands‑On Maxim Lapan Deep RL in PyTorch: cross-entropy, DQN, policy gradients, actor-critic.github.com↗
  • Graphics 3D Computer Graphics Alan Watt Modelling and rendering in 3D: transformations, shading, ray tracing, textures.amazon.com↗
